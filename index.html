What This Teaches

Each result links an analysis step to why it matters in medical research.

GC Content

GC-rich regions can affect sequencing coverage, PCR behavior, and gene regulation. Extreme values are a useful first quality signal.

ORFs and Translation

Open reading frames help identify protein-coding potential and show how nucleotide changes can alter amino acid sequences.

Variant Effect

Missense, nonsense, and silent changes are basic categories used before deeper annotation with population frequency and clinical evidence.

Expression

Differential expression helps researchers find genes and pathways that behave differently between disease and control samples.
